# Educator guide: Ten Little Seeds

## Purpose and prerequisites

Explore numerals and collections from 0 to 10; see ten as two parts; model addition and taking away within ten. The intended context is approximately age six, with individual variation. Familiarity with counting small objects and the words “more”, “fewer” and “empty” helps. Independent reading is not required if an adult reads with the learner. This is practice software, not a diagnostic assessment or a validated intervention.

## Suggested 8–12 minute visit

The timing is a flexible adult planning suggestion. There is no countdown, score ranking, failure limit or reward for speed.

1. **Look together (about 1 minute).** Begin with the example: three original seeds plus two added seeds make five. Point to each object before reading `3 + 2 = 5`. The two groups have different colours and different centre shapes. Close with “My turn”.
2. **Grow a number (about 2 minutes).** Targets are 0, 3, 6 and 10. Tap or keyboard-activate spaces to plant/lift seeds, or use Plant one/Lift one. Count aloud. The numeral and count description update with each change. Deliberately pause at zero: an empty collection is a valid answer, checked with the same button.
3. **Make ten (about 2–3 minutes).** Starting groups are 0, 5, 7, 2 and 9, producing bonds 0+10, 5+5, 7+3, 2+8 and 9+1. Original seeds stay fixed so the starting part remains visible. Added seeds can be lifted and placed in another empty slot with two taps; dragging is not needed. Correctness uses the count, not a prescribed placement. Ask “How many spaces are empty?” and “What are the two parts?” before naming the equation.
4. **Garden stories (about 3–4 minutes).** Six original situations include 2+3, 9−4, 0+4, 6−6, 4+6 and 8−3. Ask the child to enact each story. The displayed equation describes the *current model*, so an incomplete model is not presented as the correct answer. Check compares it with the story's intended result and offers a counting strategy when they differ.
5. **Fresh application (about 1–2 minutes).** Start with seven. First fill ten; a successful check acknowledges 7+3=10 and starts the second phase. Give two away and check eight. The final explanation connects both steps: 7+3=10, then 10−2=8. This combines composing ten and taking away; it differs from the opening example.

The completion summary reports how many distinct discoveries were checked, including if someone used navigation to sample rather than finish the whole sequence. It offers a concrete next practice idea without ranking the child.

## Adapt the support

- **Show the steps:** a relevant strategy remains visible beside the problem. Read it together, touch each counted object, and discuss the two groups.
- **Let me explore:** the same activities stay available; the strategy is one Hint press away. The choice is freely changeable and does not label ability.
- Use native Tab/Shift+Tab and Enter/Space or touch. Plant/Lift buttons avoid precision targeting. No dragging, timing or audio is required.
- In a pair, one person tells the story while the other moves seeds; switch roles. Ask how another arrangement could show the same quantity.
- Stop after a few problems if needed. Replay is unlimited. Make “zero” concrete by comparing an empty frame with a frame holding one seed.

## Model limitations, privacy and access

The ten-frame is a simplified two-row picture model, not a full garden simulation. Addition keeps the starting set; subtraction offers only the original members to remove or restore. This supports the stated stories while leaving other interpretations of arithmetic for later teaching. Slot numbers are positions, not quantities to sum.

Progress is memory-only and resets on refresh, closing or Start over. There is no login, child name, recording, analytics, storage, chat, payment or remote grading. Optional narration is user-initiated and plays bundled MP3 recordings of the main instructions. A Stop reading button is available, and navigation stops the prior clip. The synthetic voice was generated locally with eSpeak NG; the engine is not bundled. Failed or blocked playback leaves the visible words and adult-reading option available. Titles, hints and dynamic feedback are not prerecorded. No testing with children is needed or represented.

## Offline follow-up

Draw two rows of five boxes on paper. Use ten safe, age-appropriate objects with adult supervision. Make two groups in different ways, say their sizes, then put them together. Hide one group under a cup and ask how many objects are hidden. Finally tell a new “some arrived / some left” story and let the learner act it out.
