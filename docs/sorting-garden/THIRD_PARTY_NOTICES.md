## Bundled narration

The twelve original prompt scripts were synthesized locally with **eSpeak NG 1.52.0**, using its built-in formant voice **en-us+f3**, at 140 words per minute, pitch 60 and amplitude 85. WAV export: 22,050 Hz mono signed 16-bit PCM. Exact scripts, durations, checksums and signal checks are in `assets/audio/manifest.json`. The generated audio is supplied under `assets/audio/LICENSE.txt`, to the extent copyright exists. No cloned person, paid voice service, macOS voice, separate voice model, MBROLA asset or third-party sample recording is included.
